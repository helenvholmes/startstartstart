Privacy

Updated 20 November 2026

The short version: startstartstart! doesn't collect, keep or sell anything about you. The skills are instructions for Claude with no service behind them, and this website counts visits without cookies.

The skills and the Claude plugin

When you build a site with startstartstart!, everything happens in your own Claude session, on your own computer and in your own accounts. There's no startstartstart! server, so nothing you do is sent to me.

  • What Claude reads: your name and email (to set up git and sign your saved work), your GitHub username, and anything you share for the site, such as your resume or case studies. It uses these to build your site, which stays in the folder you chose and the GitHub repository you own.
  • Where your work goes: to GitHub and Vercel, through your own sign-ins, and the packages your site needs come from npm. Each of those has its own privacy policy.
  • Your Claude conversation is covered by Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Passwords and tokens are never asked for in the chat. Every sign-in happens on the service's own website.

This website

  • Visit counts: the site uses Vercel Web Analytics to count page views, which page sent you here, and your country and type of device. It doesn't use cookies or store anything that identifies you.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps the usual server logs under its own privacy policy.
  • Nothing else: no ads, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts, and no accounts.
  • The example sites are made up. Their contact forms don't send anything.

The showcase

If you submit your site to the showcase, you do it through a public GitHub issue, so what you write there is public. If it's added, the showcase shows your name, what you do, a screenshot of your home page and a link. To have it taken down, comment on your issue or open a new one.
